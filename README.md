# Limes Heurística – A Critical Decision Framework

**A decision analysis model born from philosophical and civic resistance.**

The *Limes Heurística* is not just a formula — it's a structured response to the erosion of public discourse, the rise of manipulative narratives, and the collapse of ethical decision-making. It emerged from conflict, not abstraction.

## 📘 What is it?
A probabilistic model for evaluating competing hypotheses in complex, ambiguous contexts. Designed to make reasoning visible, ethical, and replicable — even in politically saturated environments.

## 🔬 Core Formula

\[
P(H_i) = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{n} w_{ij} \cdot f_{ij}}{\sum_{i=1}^{2} \sum_{j=1}^{n} w_{ij} \cdot f_{ij}}
\]

- `P(Hᵢ)`: Relative plausibility of hypothesis *i*.
- `fᵢⱼ`: Degree to which factor *j* is present in hypothesis *i* (0 to 1).
- `wᵢⱼ`: Weight assigned to the importance of factor *j* for hypothesis *i* (0 to 1).
- `n`: Number of considered factors.

More than math, this is structured philosophy in motion: epistemology, ethics, and implicit ontology embedded in one formal expression.
